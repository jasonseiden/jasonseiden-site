No More Dandelions

The 90-Day Marketing Intervention
Fix What's Broken

No More Dandelions is a free playbook for CEOs, revenue leaders, and marketing teams who need to fix what's broken without making it worse.

Bain says 12% of corporate transformations achieve their original ambitions. McKinsey's talking about 90-day learning cycles. BCG is advocating for the marriage of wild creativity and rigorous discipline.

They've diagnosed the problem beautifully. What they haven't done is handed you the manual for fixing it.

This playbook does. It covers why most marketing transformations fail, starting with dandelioning, watermelon metrics, and vapor walls, and then lays out how to run an entrepreneurial intervention in a 90-day sprint, complete with real-world case studies and an execution field guide.
