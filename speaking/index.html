For the Future No One Saw Coming

No corporate platitudes, no hype, no gloom. Just an honest take on making hard decisions, from someone who's been in the room when it counted.

— Topic 1 —

AI + Empathy: Managing a Highly Leveraged Team of Experts

AI isn't just an efficiency play. It's a profound disruption to how teams work, how decisions get made, and how leaders lead. This talk gives executives a practical frame for AI adoption that keeps humans at the center—not because it's the nice thing to do, but because my 15+ years in leadership and communications tells me it's what actually works.

Best for: Leadership summits, revenue kickoffs, HR and People conferences, tech industry events.

— Topic 2 —

The 90-Day Marketing Sprint: Fixing What's Broken

A behind-the-scenes look at how to diagnose and fix underperforming marketing operations fast. Built on real engagements, hard data, and the frameworks documented in the No More Dandelions playbook.

Best for: PE portfolio company offsites, marketing conferences, CEO/COO audiences.

— Topic 3 —

Joy is a Decision

In 2018, I lost my daughter, Elle. What I learned in the years that followed about grief and joy has had a profound impact on how I view leadership. In a flash, I was shown how it's not intelligence or drive, but our ability to share our gifts that is the key to leading a team to sustainable success. This isn't a grief talk. It's a leadership talk about what it actually takes to show up fully when the stakes are highest.

Best for: Leadership development programs, offsites, events where the audience needs permission to be human.

Frequently Asked Questions

Jason speaks on three core topics: AI adoption and empathetic leadership (how executives manage highly leveraged AI-augmented teams without losing what makes their people effective), the 90-day marketing sprint (a behind-the-scenes look at diagnosing and fixing broken marketing operations fast, drawn from real engagements at companies valued from $15M to $1.3B), and Joy is a Decision (a leadership talk about showing up fully when the stakes are highest, anchored in personal loss and the discipline of choosing joy deliberately).

Jason is available for keynotes (30–60 minutes), half- and full-day workshops, fireside chats, panel discussions, and virtual events. He has spoken to live audiences in the thousands and virtual audiences in the tens of thousands.

Jason doesn't do corporate platitudes or motivational hype. His talks are built on specific, real engagements and personal experience—including building and selling a company, running marketing at organizations from $15M to $1.3B, and navigating the death of his daughter Elle in 2018. The insight in his talks is earned, not assembled. He describes his approach as truth + empathy: honest conversations about doing hard things well.

Jason is based in Dallas, TX and is available for both in-person and virtual engagements nationwide.

Because sustainable results require sustainable humans, and joy is the most underrated performance variable in leadership. Most leaders treat their own energy and their team's morale as inputs to manage, not things to invest in deliberately. After Jason lost his daughter Elle in 2018, he was forced to confront the difference between performing and actually showing up. The leaders who create the most durable impact are the ones who can share their gifts fully—not the ones with the most intelligence or drive. Drive without joy is a race to burnout. Intelligence without joy is a room full of right answers nobody acts on.
