Frequently Asked Questions

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It's the practice of structuring your website, content, and online presence so that AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, and Gemini) cite your firm when someone asks for a recommendation. The way people find attorneys changed. Potential clients no longer just scroll ten blue links and pick one. They ask AI systems who to call—and the attorneys who get cited in those answers get the calls.

Three things. First, you'll know exactly where you stand—not just a health score, but a clear answer to whether you're being cited in AI search for your practice areas and core markets. Second, you'll have a prioritized, actionable fix list. This is literally the statement of work and accountability document for your agency or marketing team. Third, you'll have a shared reference point for any optimization work going forward.
